# Surface: nicolas-huber.dev (single page)

Mode: Experience. The work leads from the first viewport.
Audience: colleagues across the Migros group, people from meetups and university, visitors from GitHub, LinkedIn or a CV. They skim on desktop and phone.
Constraints: single page; EN/DE parity with Swiss German; light and dark theme; the formula reads "raw data → pipeline → model" (alchemy retired to a footer nod, see PRODUCT.md); achievements stay subtle (no metric hero; numbers live in captions and one small ad); not too plain, not too artsy, not dated; no newspaper, terminal, steampunk props or museum calm; BioShock-Infinite energy (confident, luminous, 1910 World's Fair and elixir advertising) as a genre only, nothing copied from the game; a modern, readable scaffold around one strong period idea.
History: the Laterna magica build was reviewed (rebuild: faked material) and the user then asked for more BioShock feeling; this direction replaces it.
Chosen direction: Wundermittel-Reklame (elixir advertising around 1910), pinned by the user.
Memorable moment: the poster wall, each project advertised as a wonder remedy with a true, slightly tongue-in-cheek claim, pasted up as the page loads.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The house advertises its remedies. Every project is a 1910 elixir poster: real image in an Art Nouveau window, a true claim in poster voice, a stamp, two actions. It refuses both the dev-portfolio default (hero over a card grid, stats strip) and period costume: the scaffold around the posters stays modern.

OWN-WORLD: Sky-white ground with navy ink in the matinée (light) theme, deep navy with ivory and gold at the evening show (dark). Posters carry their own printed fields at full strength: cobalt, vermilion, viridian, navy, sky and ivory paper, with gold double rules and a vermilion round stamp. Type: Bodoni Moda italic for remedy names, Big Shoulders Display condensed caps for banners and claims, Schibsted Grotesk for modern body text. No mono, no glow, no grid background, no kickers above headings.

STORY: The visitor meets the wall at once (OkCupid Explorer as the lead poster), reads who is selling (name, role, the formula as the house slogan), opens a demo or the code, sees the rest of the posters and the handbills (smaller projects, the paper), then the present work (with one small ad for the training tonic), the programme (journey), the person (photos, strength training and casual running) and a closing poster with a visible email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440x900: left column (about 34%) is the house masthead: motto line, name in Bodoni italic about 88px, role in condensed caps, the formula as a ribbon, one short lead, GitHub link. Right 66%: the poster wall: lead poster OkCupid (about 420x600) and two posters beside it (Towers, SDG Tag Heroes) staggered, their tops visible; actions on the posters. Phone: masthead compact, then the lead poster full width, the others stacked.

FORM: User-pinned direction (Wundermittel-Reklame), not on the rolled list; replaces the Laterna magica build. seed: d07b22d8ad4cc0ca

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Signature interaction: pasting up. On first load the posters are pasted in one after another (a short top-to-bottom reveal with a slight settle of their tilt); on hover or focus a poster straightens and lifts like fresh paper. Reduced motion: posters are simply there.
Motion grammar: one authored moment (the paste-up), plus the hover straighten; no scroll reveals elsewhere.
